Write to **[oleksandr.solokha@gmail.com](mailto:oleksandr.solokha@gmail.com?subject=Kitway%20Support)**. I read every message and usually answer within a few days. In the app you can also use Kitway → Settings → Support → "Contact us".

## Purchases

- **Restore purchases:** Kitway → Settings → Kitway Premium → "Restore purchases". Use the same Apple Account that made the purchase.
- **Cancel or change a subscription:** iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Kitway. You keep Premium until the end of the paid period.
- **Refunds** are handled by Apple: [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

## Your data

- **Export:** Kitway → Settings → Privacy & permissions → Your data → "Export my data".
- **Delete everything:** Kitway → Settings → Privacy & permissions → Your data → "Delete all data".
- **Usage data and crash reports:** turn off "Share usage data" in Kitway → Settings → Privacy & permissions.

Details: [Privacy Policy](/privacy/).

## Something does not work

Please write what you did, what you expected and what happened. Add the iPhone model, the iOS version and the Kitway version (Kitway → Settings → About → Version). A screenshot helps a lot.
